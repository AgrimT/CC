STEP 1: Create Salesforce Developer Account

1. Open browser → go to developer.salesforce.com/signup
2. Fill the form:
   - First Name, Last Name
   - Email (put college email)
   - Role → select **Developer**
   - Company → write your college name
   - Country → India
   - Username → must be email format (e.g., yourname@college.dev)
3. Click **Sign Me Up**
4. Go to your email inbox → open Salesforce email → click **Verify Account**
5. Set a new password → click **Change Password**
6. You are now logged into your **Salesforce Developer Org**



STEP 2: Go to Setup

1. After login, you will see the Salesforce home screen
2. Look at the **top right corner** → click the **Gear icon ⚙️**
3. From dropdown → click **Setup**
4. Setup page will open — this is where you do everything



STEP 3: Create a Lightning App

1. In Setup, look at the **left side search bar** → type **App Manager**
2. Click **App Manager** from results
3. On the top right → click **New Lightning App** button
4. Fill in:
   - App Name: `Library Management System`
   - Developer Name: auto fills
   - Description: Library app for students and books
5. Click **Next**
6. On Console Navigation screen → click **Next**
7. On Utility Items screen → click **Next**
8. On Navigation Items → skip for now → click **Next**
9. On User Profiles → select **System Administrator**
10. Click **Save & Finish**



STEP 4: Create Custom Object — "Book"

1. In Setup search bar → type **Object Manager** → click it
2. Click **Create** (top right dropdown) → select **Custom Object**
3. Fill in the details:
   - Label: `Book`
   - Plural Label: `Books`
   - Object Name: auto fills as `Book`
   - Record Name: `Book Name`
   - Data Type: **Text**
4. Scroll down → check the box **Launch New Custom Tab Wizard after saving**
5. Click **Save**
6. Tab Wizard opens:
   - Click the **Tab Style** box → pick any icon (e.g., book icon)
   - Click **Next**
   - Click **Next** again
   - Click **Save**



STEP 5: Create Custom Object — "Student"

1. Go back to **Object Manager** → click **Create → Custom Object**
2. Fill in:
   - Label: `Student_A`
   - Plural Label: `Students_A`
   - Record Name: `Student_A Name`
   - Data Type: **Text**
3. Scroll down → check **Launch New Custom Tab Wizard after saving**
4. Click **Save**
5. Tab Wizard:
   - Choose a tab style icon
   - Click **Next → Next → Save**


STEP 6: Add Fields to Book Object

1. Go to **Object Manager** → click **Book** from the list
2. On the left side menu → click **Fields & Relationships**
3. Click **New** button
4. **Field 1 — Author:**
   - Select **Text** → click Next
   - Field Label: `Author`
   - Length: 100
   - Click Next → Next → Save
5. **Field 2 — ISBN:**
   - Click **New** again
   - Select **Number** → click Next
   - Field Label: `ISBN`
   - Click Next → Next → Save


STEP 7: Add Fields to Student Object

1. Go to **Object Manager** → click **Student_A**
2. Click **Fields & Relationships → New**
3. **Field 1 — Roll Number:**
   - Select **Number** → Next
   - Label: `Roll Number`
   - Next → Next → Save
4. **Field 2 — Email:**
   - Click **New** → Select **Email** → Next
   - Label: `Email`
   - Next → Next → Save


STEP 8: Add Objects to Your App

1. In Setup → search **App Manager** → click it
2. Find **Library Management System** in the list
3. On the right side → click the **dropdown arrow ▼** → click **Edit**
4. On the left menu → click **Navigation Items**
5. In the search box under Available Items → search **Books** → click it → click the arrow to move it to Selected
6. Search **Students_A** → move it to Selected too
7. Also add **Dashboards** if you want
8. Click **Save**


 STEP 9: Open Your App

1. Click the **9-dot grid icon** (top left, called App Launcher)
2. In the search box → type `Library Management System`
3. Click on it → your app opens
4. You will see the top navigation bar with:
   - **Students_A | Books | Contacts | Dashboards | Entitlements**


STEP 10: Add Book Records

1. Click the **Books** tab in your app
2. Click **New** button (top right)
3. Enter Book Name: `CC` → click **Save**
4. Click **New** again
5. Enter Book Name: `CG` → click **Save**
6. You will now see 2 books listed: **CC** and **CG** 


STEP 11: Add Student Records

1. Click the **Students_A** tab
2. Click **New**
3. Enter Student Name: `Rishi` → click **Save**
4. Click **New** → Enter: `Aarush` → Save
5. Click **New** → Enter: `Agrim` → Save
6. You will now see 3 students listed 


 STEP 12: Create a Dashboard (Bonus)

1. Click **Dashboards** tab
2. Click **New Dashboard**
3. Name: `Library Dashboard`
4. Click **Create**
5. Click **+ Component**
6. Choose a report if available, or skip
7. Click **Save**
